ABOUT

When it comes to the history of the blues, to know it all is impossible. By that same token, to be completely precise about its history would be inconceivable.

The history of the blues is the history of music, and the history of music is the history of humanity. More than a genre of music, the blues has shaped everything from the way we dress to the way we love.

I’ve done my best. Rolled back the years and combed through the catalogues as meticulously as a mere mortal can. The result? My best attempt at documenting the soundtrack of our lives.

I symbolically chose to start my search in 1926, a major turning point in the history of music, and end it in 1958, when, as far as I’m concerned, it was all said and done.

I hope you enjoy this journey.
